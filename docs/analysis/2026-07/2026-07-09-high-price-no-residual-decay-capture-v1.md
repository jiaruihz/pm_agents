# High-Price NO Residual/Decay Capture v1

Status: `snapshot`

## Verdict
- Broad high-price NO residual harvest as a taker hold-to-settlement carry is **rejected at this evidence level**: forward first-cross basket at `edge_hat>=0` is ROI -2.0% with date-block CI [-3.5%, -0.1%] (significantly negative), and every wider edge threshold is worse. Do not live; do not shadow the broad basket.
- Root cause is not fees but tail pricing: global exact-failure is 6.5% at avg ask 0.946 (market residual 5.4c) — the market residual is on average **smaller** than realized exact-failure risk. In 0.85-0.90 the gap is worst (residual ~12c vs failure ~14-17% on every leg); "early decay" carry in that band loses -2% to -5% per share on hold.
- 0.85-0.90 decay is a **mark-to-market illusion**: 52-64% of d1-d4 entries later see a bid >= entry+5c, but hold-to-settlement is negative on all legs — the paper gains concentrate in rows that were winning anyway; exits do not rescue the 14-17% exact-failure tail (avg exit-gain-possible is only +2-4c against a -88c loss tail).
- The empirical cell scorer `p_hat_exact_failure` is **not usable as a trade head**: in the safest forward bucket (p_hat<=2%) realized failure is 3.5% vs avg market residual 2.1c — selection concentrates anti-edge, mirroring the v2 finding that this frame's calibrated heads underperform.
- Positive pockets exist but are small and confirmation-timed, consistent with per-poll v3: d1 NO 0.95+ with next METAR imminent (<=5 min) while at-high (25 rows, 0 failures, ROI +2.3%, CI [+1.9%, +2.7%]); d3 NO 0.90-0.95 (+1.0%, CI crosses 0); current-bracket NO at 0.99+ late lock (+0.6%). These are `shadow_candidate` material only.
- City heterogeneity is large (Beijing/Chengdu/CapeTown/Istanbul-type 0-failure cities vs Manila/Shanghai/HongKong/Busan 15-33% failure), but with 44 cities and ~7% base failure, several zero-failure cities are expected by chance — the whitelist is **hypothesis-generating only**, not deployable.
- Overlay goal (recoup other strategies' missed cost): 17 of 25 live_real losing city-days (68%) had a same-day confident residual row, and most residual legs won — but three covered days (Helsinki 7/04, Busan 7/04, 7/07) lost more per share than they recouped. A negative-EV overlay does not raise portfolio ROI; only the confirmation-timed pockets above could.
- Conclusion: `inconclusive_research_shadow_only` for the pockets; `rejected` for the broad ask>=0.85 taker carry. Next step if pursued: fold residual capture into v3's late-confirmed policies (d1 imminent-obs gate, d3 residual), evaluate maker-side fill reality, and pre-register a small city whitelist for forward shadow instead of post-hoc slicing.

## Definitions
- `market_residual = 1 - NO ask`; `exact_failure = final high settles exactly in bracket K`.

- `edge_hat = (1 - p_hat) - (ask + fee)`; fee = 0.05*p*(1-p) taker.
- First-cross grain: first snapshot a market enters each price band; later re-prices are excluded from headline replay.
